Enderun Experience

Reflections, learnings, and unforgettable moments.

The Enderun Experience taught me a lot of things beyond the four corners of the classroom. A lot of topics that could help me in my daily life as a student as well as my future endeavor and as a public servant. It gave me the opportunity to learn valuable lessons that cannot always be found in books or lectures. Throughout the experience, I was able to learn more about businesses, teamwork, and leadership. I also learned the importance of discipline, responsibility, and time management in accomplishing tasks effectively.

The activities and discussions helped me become more aware of the different challenges faced by communities and how businesses work nowadays featuring newest trends. I realized that serving the people requires patience, dedication, honesty, and compassion. The experience also inspired me to become more active and financially aware.

Moreover, the Enderun Experience allowed me to meet new people, build friendships, and share ideas with others who have different perspectives in life. These meaningful experiences motivated me to strive harder in my studies and prepare myself for future responsibilities. Overall, the Enderun Experience became an unforgettable journey that shaped my character and inspired me to become a better individual and future public servant.

One of the seminars I attended that made the greatest impact on me was the “Surfs Up to Success” seminar held on May 06, 2026. This talk featured some of the country’s finest companies such as Universal Robina Corporation, Raffles Makati, and Fairmont Makati, which shared their inspiring success stories, business journeys, and the ways they successfully built their brands. Listening to their experiences motivated me to work harder and believe that success can be achieved through perseverance, dedication, and passion.

What made the seminar meaningful to me was the valuable lessons shared by the speakers about leadership, innovation, customer service, and professionalism. They explained that building a successful company does not happen overnight because it requires commitment, patience, and continuous improvement. I was inspired by how these companies overcame challenges and maintained their reputation in their respective industries.

The seminar also taught me the importance of having a clear vision and strong determination in achieving my goals. As a student and future public servant, I realized that success is not only about personal achievements but also about making a positive impact on other people’s lives.

I actively participated in each of these memorable activities by attending the seminars and talks with enthusiasm, interest, and an open mind. Participating in these events gave me motivation to continue striving hard in my studies and to pursue my personal goals in life. I always looked forward to joining these seminars because they provided me with valuable knowledge, inspiration, and opportunities to learn from experienced speakers and professionals.

During the activities, I listened carefully to the discussions and took down important notes that I could apply in my academic journey and future career. I also participated by asking questions, sharing my thoughts, and engaging in conversations with my fellow students and the guest speakers. These interactions helped me gain confidence in expressing my ideas and opinions while also learning from the perspectives of others.

Moreover, I actively cooperated in group activities and discussions whenever there were collaborative tasks during the seminars. I enjoyed exchanging ideas and experiences with other participants because it allowed me to broaden my understanding of different topics. Through my active participation, I realized the importance of communication, teamwork, and continuous learning. Overall, these memorable activities became meaningful experiences that inspired me to become more motivated, responsible, and goal-oriented in both my studies and personal life.

The different seminars and talks such as “Building a Career in Global Finance,” “Surfs Up to Success,” “The Better You,” and the “Overview Internship Seminar” provided me with valuable insights and important life skills that can help me in both my studies and future career. These activities taught me the importance of professionalism, confidence, communication, and continuous self-improvement in achieving success.

The seminar about building a career in global finance helped me understand the significance of financial literacy, proper decision-making, and career planning. It also inspired me to become more responsible in managing resources and preparing for future opportunities. Meanwhile, “Surfs Up to Success” taught me lessons about perseverance, leadership, innovation, and how successful companies build strong reputations through hard work and dedication.

“The Better You” seminar encouraged me to improve myself personally and emotionally by developing self-confidence, discipline, and a positive mindset. On the other hand, the “Overview Internship Seminar” prepared me for real-world experiences by teaching workplace ethics, teamwork, adaptability, and professionalism.

Overall, these seminars and talks enhanced my knowledge, strengthened my interpersonal skills, and motivated me to become more determined in reaching my goals. They also prepared me to face future challenges as a student, professional, and responsible public servant.

My overall takeaway from my time in Enderun is that learning goes beyond academics and classroom discussions. The experience helped me grow not only as a student but also as an individual who is preparing for future responsibilities and challenges. Through the different seminars, activities, and interactions with speakers and fellow students, I gained valuable knowledge, practical skills, and life lessons that I can apply in my personal and professional life.

One of the most important lessons I learned is the value of discipline, hard work, and perseverance in achieving success. The talks and seminars inspired me to become more confident, goal-oriented, and determined to pursue my dreams despite the challenges that may come along the way. I also realized the importance of communication, teamwork, leadership, and professionalism in building good relationships and becoming effective in any field or career.

Moreover, my time in Enderun encouraged me to become more open-minded and eager to continuously improve myself. It motivated me to become more responsible and prepared for future opportunities, especially in serving others as a future public servant. Overall, the Enderun experience became a meaningful and unforgettable journey that shaped my character, broadened my perspective, and inspired me to strive for excellence in everything I do.

Future Aspirations

Reflections and dreams for the years ahead.

10 Years From Now

There’s a saying, “Live while you’re young,” which is about enjoying life and making the most of the present. Entering my mid-20s is exciting, but it also makes me think about my future. As a student and, at the same time, a public servant, I see myself ten years from now as a leader whom people can rely on, a leader who always seeks the welfare of my constituents.

Ten years from now, I want to see myself serving a community, doing my best to provide the help and services that people deserve. The responsibility I have now to lead my fellow youth is one of the foundations that will help me become the leader I aspire to be in the future. It has always been my dream to lead a community where I can work closely with the people and serve them wholeheartedly.

To my future self ten years from now, I hope you are enjoying life while being responsible enough to fulfill your duties. I want you to know that your younger self from ten years ago is proud of where you are today. I hope you have touched many people’s lives and never grow tired of doing what you love most, leading and serving others.

I also want you to remember that if you make mistakes, it’s okay because they are part of your learning journey. I hope you are braver now than you were before. I wish you nothing but the best.

30 Years From Now

Every milestone in our lives should be celebrated, no matter what we have achieved. Thinking about your future 30 years from now may seem too far ahead, but for me, thinking about it now gives me the determination to keep doing my best in everything I do. Thirty years from now, I see myself as a successful leader in our community, a responsible father, and a loving husband.

As a successful leader 30 years from now, I hope I will have provided my community with the services and support they need. I also hope to see people united through my leadership and the youth actively engaging in leadership as well. Thirty years from now, I wish to be a leader who has helped build a peaceful community where people are not afraid of their leaders and feel comfortable expressing their needs and concerns. At 54 years old, before I pass the responsibility of leadership to the younger generation, I hope I will have left behind a legacy they will never forget.

I also hope that 30 years from now, I will have established a successful business that provides for my family. I want it to be a business that creates jobs in our community and offers fair compensation that truly reflects the value of people’s work.

To my future self 30 years from now, I hope you enjoyed your years of serving the community. I just want to tell you that making it this far is what truly defines your success. I hope that leading others while building your own family has brought you happiness and fulfillment. In the years ahead, you may decide to retire from leadership, but I hope you continue to inspire others through the legacy you have built in your community.

As the head of your family, I hope you were able to give them not only your support but also your time and presence, so they always felt your love. I know you can achieve all of these dreams, and that is why I am already so proud of you.

Career Aspirations

My next steps after college and my target profession.

Graduating from college is not the end of my journey but the beginning of a greater responsibility. As I prepare to complete my education, I see myself stepping into a future filled with opportunities to serve, grow, and make a positive impact in the lives of others. Throughout my years as a student and a public servant, I have learned that success is not measured only by personal achievements but also by the difference we make in our communities. These experiences have shaped my goals and strengthened my desire to become a leader who serves with integrity, compassion, and dedication.

My next steps after college are centered on continuing my journey in public service while pursuing professional growth. I plan to gain more experience in governance, community development, and public administration. I believe that learning does not stop after graduation, so I intend to participate in seminars, leadership training programs, and other opportunities that will enhance my knowledge and skills. If given the opportunity, I also hope to pursue graduate studies in the future to further equip myself with the competencies needed to become an effective public servant and leader.

At the same time, I want to continue serving the youth and my community. My involvement in youth leadership has taught me the importance of listening to people, understanding their needs, and working together to find meaningful solutions. These experiences have inspired me to remain active in community development and local governance. I want to continue creating programs that empower young people, encourage civic participation, and promote leadership among the next generation. I believe that investing in the youth today will help build stronger and more resilient communities tomorrow.

My target profession is to become a public servant and community leader who genuinely works for the welfare of the people. Leadership, for me, is not about holding a position or gaining recognition. It is about being responsible, trustworthy, and committed to improving the lives of others. I aspire to become a leader whom people can rely on, especially during times of difficulty. I want to be someone who listens to the concerns of the community, makes informed decisions, and ensures that public service reaches those who need it most.

One of my greatest career aspirations is to contribute to building a peaceful, progressive, and inclusive community. I dream of becoming a leader who promotes transparency, accountability, and unity among the people. I hope to create programs that improve access to education, livelihood opportunities, healthcare, youth development, and other essential services. I want every individual in the community to feel heard, valued, and supported. More importantly, I want to inspire others, especially young people, to become active participants in nation-building and community development.

Aside from serving in government, I also aspire to become an entrepreneur. In the future, I hope to establish a successful business that not only provides financial stability for my family but also creates employment opportunities within our community. I believe that businesses have the power to contribute to economic growth and improve the quality of life of many people. Through entrepreneurship, I hope to provide decent jobs, fair compensation, and a positive working environment that values the dignity and potential of every employee. Combining public service and entrepreneurship will allow me to create a greater impact by addressing both social and economic needs.

As I continue pursuing my career, I also aspire to build a strong and loving family. I believe that success is not only measured by professional accomplishments but also by the relationships we nurture. In the future, I hope to become a responsible husband and a loving father who provides guidance, support, and unconditional love to my family. I want to balance my responsibilities as a leader with my responsibilities at home because I believe that true leadership begins with the values we practice within our own family.

Looking ahead ten years from now, I envision myself as a trusted leader who continues to serve with humility and dedication. I hope to have earned the trust of the people through honest leadership and meaningful service. Thirty years from now, I hope to leave behind a legacy that future generations can be proud of—a legacy of integrity, compassion, and genuine public service. Before passing the responsibility of leadership to younger individuals, I want to ensure that I have inspired them to continue serving with the same passion and commitment.

I understand that achieving these aspirations will not be easy. There will be challenges, setbacks, and moments of uncertainty. However, I believe that every obstacle is an opportunity to learn and become stronger. Mistakes are part of growth, and every experience will help shape me into a better leader and a better person. With determination, perseverance, and faith, I am confident that I can overcome these challenges while remaining true to my values and purpose.

In conclusion, my next steps after college involve continuing my journey of learning, serving, and leading. My target profession is to become a dedicated public servant who works tirelessly for the welfare of the people while also pursuing entrepreneurship to create opportunities for others. My career aspirations are rooted in service, leadership, and community development. More than achieving personal success, I want to make a lasting difference in the lives of the people I serve. I hope to become a leader who leads with integrity, empowers others, strengthens communities, and leaves behind a meaningful legacy for future generations. Ultimately, I want my life to reflect the belief that true success is found not only in what we achieve for ourselves but in how we uplift and inspire others along the way.
